An example book note. It has a cover image, a rating and notes, so it gets its own page and a place on the Library shelf.

Twelve short books of notes Marcus Aurelius wrote to himself, never meant for anyone else. Read it slowly, a few entries at a time; it rewards being picked up rather than read through.

## What stayed with me

- He keeps reminding himself of the same few things, which makes it feel like a real notebook and not a treatise.
- Most entries are about how to meet other people’s bad behaviour without becoming bitter.
- He is harder on himself than on anyone else.

> Begin the morning by saying to thyself, I shall meet with the busy-body, the ungrateful, arrogant, deceitful, envious, unsocial.

The quote is from George Long’s 1862 translation, which is in the public domain. More modern translations read more easily.
