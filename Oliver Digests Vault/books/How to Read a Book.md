An example book note with no cover image, so the Library shows it in a plain cloth binding.

Adler describes four levels of reading, each including the ones before it:

1. **Elementary:** understanding what the sentences say.
2. **Inspectional:** skimming to learn what a book is about and whether it deserves a closer read.
3. **Analytical:** reading thoroughly, asking what the book says, whether it’s true, and why it matters.
4. **Syntopical:** reading several books on one subject against each other.

The advice I actually use: before reading a book closely, spend ten minutes with its contents page, introduction and conclusion.
